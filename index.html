? 02 / QUESTIONS

  1. How can we maximise the value of human wisdom as machines increasingly automate our work and thinking?
  2. How can we build trust into complex, powerful systems while improving public understanding of their capabilities and risks?
  3. How can people who have never quite fitted in find safety and connection without feeling pressured to perform or compromise on their real needs and values?

! 03 / CORE VALUES

Care over consumption

Our time and attention are finite. I want to use mine to make cool things, help people, and take part in the world, rather than hand it to systems and tech designed to keep us passive and consuming.

Autonomy over autopilot

Technology is becoming exponentially better at hijacking attention, desire, and ultimately attachment. People need low-cost, easy-to-use tools and space to notice what is shaping them. My hope is that, through fighting fire with fire, we can use the same powerful technologies to empower people to make their own choices and live in alignment with their own values.

Honest curiosity over comforting stories

Looking properly at the world and ourselves, and staying open long enough to be changed by what we find. Exploring how our brains predict and interpret the world, IFS therapy and embracing neurodivergence have all helped me make sense of attention, motivation and inner conflict.

Project

ControlPlane

CybersecurityProfessional work

Helping teams understand and secure critical systems.

I'm a cybersecurity engineer with hands-on penetration testing and threat-modelling experience across highly regulated environments. I've helped clients secure everything from mainframes and traditional infrastructure through to cloud-native platforms, Kubernetes clusters, and agentic systems. Most recently, I've been building and assessing AI security capabilities, including harnesses, skill-ingestion controls, and evaluation pipelines.

The approach

How can we continue to secure and trust critical systems as they become more powerful and opaque?

Using an attacker's mindset, I help clients and end-users build and trust complex systems with little room for error, making their capabilities and risks easier to understand.

character-sheet

FreeOpen sourceGamified dashboard

Turn goals into quests and make your progress visible.

A free, privacy-first life HUD with journalling, XP and achievements. Use it with or without AI, without reducing your life to a productivity score.

The idea behind it

What could help empower humans to understand ourselves, appreciate our growth, and find balance in an age where distraction, avoidance, and obsessive self-improvement have never been easier?

A customisable gamification framework that makes journalling, with or without AI, feel like it is building towards something real. Seeing neglected stats brings attention back to what matters to you over the long term.

Demo release in preparation

The Treasury

FreeOpen sourcePersonal finance

See where your money goes and what a typical month leaves available.

Income, spending, savings and investments in one view. Explore the payments behind a category and compare months without living in a spreadsheet.

Run it locally in your browser with your own exports, without an account or bank connection. The public example is entirely fictional, and the visual styles range from a clean dashboard to a medieval treasury.

Science fictionIn progress

Two outsiders trying to choose a life they no longer want to escape from.

An in-progress science-fiction novel about Max and Wren. One grew up relying on AI companions and virtual escapism; the other was forced onto a path of spiritual enlightenment and self-reliance. Their story explores purpose, dependence, manipulation and connection.

The question behind the story
