I'm a cyber security engineer with hands-on experience in penetration testing and threat modelling across large financial organisations and other highly regulated environments. I've helped clients secure everything from mainframes and on-premises infrastructure through to cloud-native platforms, Kubernetes clusters, and agentic systems.

Most recently, I've been working on agentic threat modelling, building custom AI harnesses, skill-ingestion controls and evaluation pipelines to build deterministic trust into agents and systems built by agents.

04 / Questions

  1. How can we maximise human wisdom as machines increasingly automate our work and thinking?
  2. How can we build trust into complex, powerful systems while improving public understanding of their capabilities and risks?
  3. How can people who never feel like they fit in find connection without feeling pressured to perform or compromise their real values and needs?

05 / Core values

Care >over consumption

Our time and attention are finite.

I want to use mine to make cool things, help people, and take part in the world, rather than hand it to systems and tech designed to keep us passive and consuming.

Autonomy >over autopilot

Technology is becoming exponentially better at hijacking attention, desire, and ultimately attachment. People need low-cost, easy-to-use tools and space to notice what is shaping them.

My hope is that, through fighting fire with fire, we can use the same powerful technologies to empower people to make their own choices and live in alignment with their own values.

Honest curiosity >over comforting stories

Looking properly at the world and ourselves, and staying open long enough to be changed by what we find.

Exploring how our brains predict and interpret the world, IFS therapy and embracing neurodivergence have all helped me make sense of attention, motivation and inner conflict.
